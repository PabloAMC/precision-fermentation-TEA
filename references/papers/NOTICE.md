# Third-party files in this folder

`Humbird2021_cultured_meat_SI.xlsm` is the supplementary workbook of

> Humbird D. Scale-up economics for cultured meat. *Biotechnology and Bioengineering*
> 118(8):3239–3250 (2021). doi:10.1002/bit.27848

The article and its supplementary material are open access under a Creative Commons
Attribution (CC BY) licence (Europe PMC, PMC8362201). It is redistributed here unmodified,
with attribution, because `src/pftea/humbird_yeast.py` ports it and `tests/` check the port
against it. All other files in `references/` (PDFs, text dumps) are not redistributed; see
`references/INDEX.md` for where to obtain them.

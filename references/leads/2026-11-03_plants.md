## How this was read (check this before using any number)

- WebFetch and curl were blocked in this session: every page host failed DNS or got a proxy 403
  (novonesis.com, nebraska.gov, newstribune.com, nne.com). **No page was opened directly.** Every
  "quote" below is text that the WebSearch tool returned as the page's own words, after it passed
  through a summarising language model. Treat each as a pointer: open the URL and re-check the exact
  words before you copy anything into data/evidence/plants.yaml.
- I did not estimate, convert or derive any number. Where a value is missing it says "not found".
- Existing entries in data/evidence/plants.yaml (Liberation Labs, Solar Foods F02, Quorn Billingham,
  Angel Yeast Yichang 970.45M CNY) are not repeated, except for an update on Liberation Labs at the end.
- Scope labels: greenfield = new plant on a new site; expansion = added capacity at an operating
  site; brownfield = reuse of an existing building or equipment; co-located = new plant beside another

### 1. novozymes_blair_2012
- plant: Novozymes enzyme plant, Blair, Nebraska, USA (owner Novozymes, now Novonesis)
- status: operating (inaugurated 30 May 2012)
- capex: about 200,000,000
- currency_year: USD, 2009-2012 nominal (as stated at completion)

- volume_m3: 1,600 (stated by engineering contractor NNE: total fermentation capacity; vessel count/size not found)
- output_t_yr: not found
- product: industrial enzymes (liquid, for corn ethanol)
- url: https://nne.com/projects/building-the-worlds-most-advanced-enzyme-plant-for-biofuel/ (volume);
  https://www.governorsbiofuelscoalition.org/novozymes-opens-200m-neb-enzyme-plant/ (cost);
  https://www.bionity.com/en/news/138164/novozymes-inaugurates-largest-enzyme-plant-dedicated-to-biofuels-in-united-states.html (cost, date)
- date: 2012-05-30 (opening); NNE page undated
- quote: NNE: "increases Novozymes' global production capacity with 1,600 m3 of fermentation capacity".
  Opening coverage: the investment was "USD 200 million" across "nine buildings" (search extract).
- note: **Best enzyme datapoint found.** The cost and the volume come from different sources (Novozymes or trade
  press for cost, the contractor for volume). Product is a low-purity liquid enzyme, so downstream processing (DSP)
  is much simpler than for food-grade protein powder. An enzyme plant is close to a precision-fermentation
  plant on the fermentation side and lighter on DSP.

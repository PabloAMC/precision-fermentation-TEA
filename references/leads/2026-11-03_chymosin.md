# Research: fermentation-produced chymosin (FPC) as a market check for secreted food proteins

Researched 2026-11-03.

## Read this first: how the evidence was collected

- **Page fetching was blocked in this session.** WebFetch and curl could not reach
  `patents.google.com` or `image-ppubs.uspto.gov` (host not allowed / proxy 403), so I stopped
  fetching pages. Every entry below comes from **web-search result excerpts**: the search tool
  returned short quoted passages next to each URL. None of these pages was opened.
- So a "quote" below is the passage the search tool showed for that URL. The locator is
  "search excerpt" unless the excerpt itself gave a section or table number. **Check each quote
  against the live page before it goes into data/pf/base.yaml.**
- `kind` values: `primary` (patent, paper, regulator), `distributor_retail` (small-pack shop
  price), `distributor_bulk` (a 20 L drum or similar from a shop), `trade_stat` (customs unit
  value), `market_report` (paid market research; weak), `secondary` (encyclopedia or review).
- I computed no source values. The section "Derived arithmetic" at the end shows unit
  conversions done on quoted values. Those are labelled as derived and should be checked.

## 1. Specific activity and IMCU-to-mass conversion

### chy_imcu_nmol
- key: chymosin_B_nmol_per_IMCU
- value: 0.126 ("about")
- unit: nmol bovine chymosin B per IMCU
- basis: IDF IMCU, commercial bovine chymosin B (Maxiren, CHY-MAX)
- year: patents filed about 2013-2016 (exact year not verified)
- kind: primary (patent definition section)
- source: US 10,167,463 "Modified chymosin polypeptides"; same sentence in US 9,822,352 and
  US 10,253,305 "Chymosine enzyme variants"; also US 11,629,343 and US 10,941,389
- url: https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/10167463 (also .../9822352, .../10253305)
- locator: definitions paragraph ("The term IMCU ..."); search excerpt
- quote: "One IMCU equals about 0.126 nmol of bovine chymosin B (e.g. Maxiren or CHY-MAX)."
- note: This is the best primary basis for converting IMCU to mass. It is the patentee's own
  statement and no measurement is reported with it. The search tool found the exact sentence
  in several patent families. The assignee was not checked (the "Chymosine enzyme variants"
  family is believed to be DSM's).

### chy_idf_reference
- key: IDF_reference_standard_IMCU_per_g
- value: 1000
- unit: IMCU per g of the first calf chymosin reference standard powder
- basis: definition of the unit, IDF Standard 157A:1997
- year: 1997
- kind: primary (standard, quoted in the patents)
- source: US 10,167,463 and related patents, quoting IDF 157A:1997
- url: https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/10167463
- locator: search excerpt
- quote: "the total milk-clotting activity of the first batch of calf chymosin reference standard powder has once and for all been set at 1000 International Milk-Clotting Units per gram (IMCU/g)"

### chy_sa_B
- key: chymosin_B_specific_activity
- value: 237
- unit: IMCU per mg chymosin B

### chy_tos_ratio_efsa (activity per mg TOS of the commercial enzyme concentrate)
- key: FPC_IMCU_per_mg_TOS
- value: A. niger product 144.0 (mean); K. lactis product 12.7 (mean); one GRAS notice 62; calf
  rennet 42.8 (batch range 32.3-52.6)
- unit: IMCU per mg total organic solids (TOS)
- basis: batches of the food enzyme concentrate, before formulation
- year: 2022 (EFSA opinions); calf rennet opinion about 2022
- kind: primary (EFSA opinions, FDA GRAS notice)
- source: EFSA J. 2022;20(8):7464 (A. niger DSM 29544, Chr. Hansen); EFSA J. 2022;20(8):7462
  (K. lactis CHY, DSM); EFSA calf rennet opinion (PMC9208323); an FDA GRAS notice on
  regulations.gov
- url: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9358533/ ; https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/efsajournal/pub/7462 ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9208323/ ; https://downloads.regulations.gov/FDA-2026-N-1832-0063/attachment_1.pdf
- locator: search excerpts. Which value belongs to which document was attributed by the search
  tool and **was not verified**.
- quote: "The chymosin activity is quantified relative to an enzyme standard and expressed in International Milk Clotting Unit/g (IMCU/g)."; "TOS calculated as 100% – % water – % ash."; calf rennet: mean about 42.8 IMCU/mg TOS, range 32.3 to 52.6
- note: TOS is not chymosin protein. TOS also contains other fermentation solids. If 144
  IMCU/mg TOS is compared with about 223-237 IMCU/mg pure chymosin, chymosin would be a large
  share of TOS in the A. niger product, and much less in the K. lactis product (12.7). That is
  an inference. A second search for the 144 and 12.7 numbers did not find them again.
  **Moderate to low confidence. Open the opinions' "specifications" tables.**

## 6. Production host, process, titer

### titer_dunncoleman1991
- key: A_awamori_chymosin_titer_commercial
- value: ">1"
- unit: g/L of secreted chymosin
- basis: A. niger var. awamori, glucoamylase-prochymosin fusion, strain improved by mutagenesis and robotic screening (Genencor, the basis of CHY-MAX)
- year: 1991
- kind: primary (paper)
- source: Dunn-Coleman N.S. et al. (1991) "Commercial levels of chymosin production by Aspergillus", Bio/Technology 9:976-981, doi:10.1038/nbt1091-976
- url: https://scilit.com/publications/772a5a5bdd35afdad53789fe222e6fc5
- locator: abstract, search excerpt
- quote: (search tool's paraphrase of the abstract) "increased the production of bovine chymosin to more than one gram per liter of secreted authentic enzyme by combining a mutagenesis protocol with a novel robotic screening program"
- note: This is the only published commercial titer found. Before the fusion construct, yields
  were "below 15 mg/l in 50 ml shake flask cultures" (Ward et al. 1990, Bio/Technology 8:435,
  doi:10.1038/nbt0590-435). Current industrial titers are not published.

> pf-tea is an open cost model for seven precision-fermented food proteins. It uses public data only, and every input carries a source, a quote and an evidence grade (A-E). It balances elements and electrons, and it checks oxygen transfer, heat removal and vessel volume, so it never costs a fermenter that could not run. For each protein it reports a base case at a design-report titer, the best published titer, the titer in companies' cost models (GFI 2025) and a physical-limit best case, with tornado and Monte Carlo charts. Before any new claim, it reproduced Humbird's baker's-yeast workbook and rebuilt NREL 2011, Intelligen 2025 and Voutilainen 2021. COP here is operating cost plus depreciation in 2025 US$ for a US Midwest plant; the model's headline price adds a 14.6%/yr capital charge. The author built it with an AI assistant, and AI reviewers audited it; no human expert has reviewed it yet.

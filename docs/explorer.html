What this is. A cost with a capital charge of 14.6% of investment a year (7.5% over 10 years), not a market price. It excludes R&D, licences, regulatory work, sales and first-of-a-kind premiums.

The oxygen limit. A 200 m³ fermenter transfers at most 150 mol O₂/m³/h. A design is marked as past the limit when the titer needs more oxygen, or more cooling, than that in the time allowed. Those prices are shown but cannot be achieved as designed.

Incumbent prices. These are export unit values from UN Comtrade and Eurostat, per kg of protein (80% protein for whey, 78% for egg white). The 2026 whey spot is from USDA Dairy Market News.

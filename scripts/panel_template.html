Can microbes make milk and egg proteins as cheaply as cows and hens?

Precision fermentation puts the gene for one animal protein into a yeast or a fungus. The microbes grow on sugar in large steel tanks and release the protein. The protein is then purified and dried to a powder. This model estimates what that costs for seven proteins. It compares each cost with the price of the same protein from animals.

Does the model get known things right?

No company publishes what its fermentation protein costs to make. So the model was tested on cases with a known answer before it was used here. A reality check compares it with a market price or a real plant. A consistency check compares it with another model. Agreeing with another model is not proof that either is right. No input was tuned to pass these tests.

What if

Titer, production time and plant size move between full model runs. Every value shown is a real run. Prices, the capital charge and the location do not change the plant. The page recomputes the cost of the same plant.

Where the money goes

Each cost is an amount per kg of protein times a unit price. You can change any price.

Why: from titer to price, step by step

The page redoes each step with the numbers shown. It then compares its result with the model's. A tick means they agree. Each link opens the source of that input.

Checks against reality

Before saying anything new, the model was run on cases with a known answer. A reality check compares it with a market price or a real plant. A consistency check compares it with another model. Agreeing with another model is not proof that either is right.
